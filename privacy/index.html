Privacy Policy

This draft describes the data flows currently implemented in Viva Antiqua. It is not ready to be submitted as a public privacy policy.

Account and learning data

Account identifiers support sign-in and account management. Learning progress, saved vocabulary, reader preferences, and related activity may be stored on your device and synchronized with the app service. Purchase and entitlement records support subscriptions and paid features.

Microphone and pronunciation

Pronunciation practice uses a recording and a transcript to compare your speech with the displayed phrase. Apple Speech can process recognition on-device when available, or through Apple’s service. Without optional training-contribution consent, the app deletes the attempt recording after scoring.

Contributing pronunciation recordings for speech-recognition development is a separate, revocable opt-in. With consent, recordings and associated metadata can be sent to the app server. Withdrawal clears queued contributions and requests deletion of contributed recordings and records. The production retention period must be set before this collection is enabled.

AI conversations

Live Balbus conversation streams microphone audio to OpenAI for real-time transcription. Text conversation is processed by the configured AI services. This audio does not enter the optional pronunciation training queue. The exact production provider list, international processing locations, and provider retention settings remain to be confirmed.

Social sharing

When available, social sharing requires you to choose a destination and authorize the relevant account. YouTube upload requests the permission needed to upload your selected video and metadata to your channel. TikTok connection transfers the selected video to your TikTok inbox so you can finish editing and publishing in TikTok.

The current TikTok implementation uses a short-lived, account-bound authorization record with a ten-minute expiry and an encrypted authorization code. Expired records are rejected; cleanup runs when new connection requests are created. Provider refresh tokens are not retained by this integration. Temporary excerpt files are created inside the app for preview and transfer.

Community and external services

Content you post to the community is shared with its intended audience. Reports and moderation processing support community operation. Content transferred to another platform is also subject to that platform’s privacy and audience settings.

Deletion and choices

The app provides account deletion and separate withdrawal of pronunciation contribution consent. Account deletion removes learning and profile data and contributed recordings; some de-identified billing records may remain where required. Backup expiry, billing retention, and support request procedures must be finalized.
