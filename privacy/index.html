Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Viva Antiqua handles information when you use its Latin and Ancient Greek learning app and community.

Accounts and learning

We process sign-in identifiers to authenticate your account. Your profile, saved vocabulary, reading progress, preferences and learning activity support the features you use and synchronization between devices. Purchase and entitlement records support subscriptions, credits and access to paid features.

Voice features and optional contributions

Voice features require microphone access. Pronunciation practice processes your recording and transcript to assess the displayed passage. Depending on the feature and device, processing uses Viva Antiqua’s speech services or Apple speech recognition. Live Balbus voice conversations send audio to OpenAI for transcription; this audio is not added to the optional pronunciation contribution queue.

Contributing pronunciation recordings to improve speech recognition is optional and separate from microphone permission. Declining does not prevent ordinary pronunciation practice. Contributions include recordings and associated transcripts and technical metadata. You can withdraw consent in Settings, which clears pending contributions and requests deletion of your stored contributions. Contributions are for speech recognition, not voice cloning or voice synthesis.

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the text, passage context or audio needed for that feature is processed by our service and the AI provider used for that request. Live transcription uses OpenAI. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in prompts or recordings.

Connect

Posts, shorts, profile information and shared stories are visible to their intended community audience. Direct messages and pen-pal letters are intended for their participants. Reports, automated safety checks and authorized moderation may involve processing submitted content. Do not assume messages are end-to-end encrypted. We process follows, reactions and saved items to provide community features, and device push tokens to deliver notifications when enabled.

Connected platforms

For TikTok book-video sharing, Viva Antiqua uses TikTok authorization and uploads the selected video directly to TikTok for you to finish editing and posting from your TikTok inbox. This flow does not save the video to Photos. Our server exchanges the authorization code; the app holds the resulting access token temporarily in memory and does not retain a refresh token. TikTok handles the video after it receives the upload. This integration is still undergoing testing and review.

For YouTube book-video sharing, Viva Antiqua asks for the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) through Google Sign-In, only when you choose to share a video to YouTube.

  • Google user data accessed: an access token for the upload permission and your basic Google profile (name and email address), which the app uses only to show which account is connected. When an upload finishes, YouTube returns the new video's ID so the app can link to it.
  • How it is used: the token is used only to upload the video you selected to your own channel, with the title, description, visibility and made-for-kids setting you chose. The app does not read, change or delete your other YouTube videos, playlists, comments or channel data.
  • Sharing: the upload goes directly from your device to Google. We do not send your Google access token, YouTube data or the uploaded video to our servers, and we do not share or sell Google user data to third parties, including advertisers and data brokers.
  • Protection: Google Sign-In stores its tokens in your device's Keychain, and connections to Google are encrypted.
  • Retention and deletion: tokens remain on your device until you sign out of Viva Antiqua, delete the app, or remove access in your Google Account connections. Videos you uploaded stay in your YouTube account, where you manage or delete them; disconnecting Viva Antiqua does not delete them, and we keep no copies of them.

Google account information and YouTube permissions are used to provide the features you request, not for advertising or sale, and are not used to develop, improve or train AI/ML models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, service providers and security

Information is stored on your device and, where required for account, synchronization and online features, on our servers. Hosting, authentication, payment, AI and destination-platform providers process information needed for their functions. Information may be processed outside your country. We use authenticated access and encrypted network connections; no system can guarantee absolute security.

We keep account and feature data while needed to provide the service. Account deletion removes or de-identifies associated records through the service’s deletion process. Certain billing, security and moderation records may remain where needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention or dispute handling. Copies in backups may persist beyond deletion from active systems; deletion from active systems is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup.

Sharing limits and security records

To prevent repeated extraction of recordings, sharing is currently available only to accounts we invite, and is limited per person: a set number of newly issued excerpts in a rolling 7-day period (currently three), shared across platforms. Accounts and devices used together are treated as one person for this limit, so switching accounts or devices does not reset it. There are also overall limits on how much audio all users together can share per day and in total. Limits use default playback time, including character names and default pauses: Stories end at a complete sentence within 60 seconds; other supported formats may finish the sentence that began before 60 seconds, up to 75 seconds. Your playback settings can change the final video duration. Timing metadata downloads with the book, and selecting an excerpt does not consume an export. An export counts when its audio excerpt is issued after you choose Export, even if you later cancel posting. Original recording URLs and decryption keys are not provided through sharing.

We retain purpose-specific, pseudonymous account and device identifiers and hourly timestamp/count summaries for excerpt issuance, share-file download requests, offline playback-license issuance, account deletion and device removal for 90 days from each event. These security records remain for that period after account deletion or removal of a registered device, then expire automatically. If we restrict sharing for an account, we keep a pseudonymous record of that restriction until we lift it, so that it also applies after the account is deleted and created again. When you accept a sharing invitation, we record that the invitation was used by your account. These records do not contain your email address, sign-in tokens, raw IP address or audio recordings. Pseudonymous identifiers can still link related activity and are not anonymous. This information is used for service security and enforcing sharing limits, not advertising.

The app keeps an installation identifier in the device Keychain to help recognize reinstalls. This is not a permanent hardware identifier and recognition is not guaranteed after device resets or changes to system storage. Share excerpts are available for authenticated re-download for 24 hours and removed from active server storage afterwards, or when the account is deleted. Already exported copies in Photos or destination platforms are outside our deletion controls. A download request or an offline-license issuance does not by itself prove a completed download or a public social-media post.

Viva Antiqua Open submissions

When you submit writing for publication we store the text, your pen name, the optional translation, your AI-use statements, the credits charged and the review outcome, linked to your account. To screen submissions we send the text, title, pen name and translation to Anthropic and OpenAI under their API terms, which do not use API data to train their models. When we publish a work, its text, pen name and narration are shown to all users. We keep which works you liked and which pen names you follow, and show only totals to others. If you withdraw a work or delete your account, the work is removed from the catalog; review records of declined or withdrawn submissions are kept for up to one year for abuse prevention and legal claims.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile, manage device permissions and notifications, revoke connected-platform access, withdraw optional recording consent and request account deletion in the app’s account settings. Deleting the app alone does not delete your server account or cancel an Apple subscription. You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify account ownership without asking for your password.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. The website does not include our own advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice or request consent where appropriate for material changes.
