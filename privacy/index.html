Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Viva Antiqua handles information when you use its Latin and Ancient Greek learning app and community.

Accounts and learning

We process sign-in identifiers to authenticate your account. Your profile, saved vocabulary, reading progress, preferences and learning activity support the features you use and synchronization between devices. Purchase and entitlement records support subscriptions, credits and access to paid features.

Voice features and optional contributions

Voice features require microphone access. Pronunciation practice processes your recording and transcript to assess the displayed passage. Depending on the feature and device, processing uses Viva Antiqua’s speech services or Apple speech recognition. Live Balbus voice conversations send audio to OpenAI for transcription; this audio is not added to the optional pronunciation contribution queue.

Contributing pronunciation recordings to improve speech recognition is optional and separate from microphone permission. Declining does not prevent ordinary pronunciation practice. Contributions include recordings and associated transcripts and technical metadata. You can withdraw consent in Settings, which clears pending contributions and requests deletion of your stored contributions. Contributions are for speech recognition, not voice cloning or voice synthesis.

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the text, passage context or audio needed for that feature is processed by our service and the AI provider used for that request. Live transcription uses OpenAI. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in prompts or recordings.

Connect

Posts, shorts, profile information and shared stories are visible to their intended community audience. Direct messages and pen-pal letters are intended for their participants. Reports, automated safety checks and authorized moderation may involve processing submitted content. Do not assume messages are end-to-end encrypted. We process follows, reactions and saved items to provide community features, and device push tokens to deliver notifications when enabled.

Connected platforms

For TikTok book-video sharing through Share Kit, Viva Antiqua requests permission to add the selected generated video to Photos and passes its local asset identifier to the installed TikTok app for editing and posting. This permission does not give Viva Antiqua access to read your photo library. The saved video remains in Photos until you remove it. This native sharing flow does not request a TikTok web login, store TikTok access or refresh tokens, or upload the shared video to our server. TikTok manages its own photo permissions and handles the video after you share it.

Where available, connecting YouTube requires your authorization. We use the permissions you grant to transfer the video and metadata you choose. Uploads and their audience settings are also governed by the destination platform. Disconnecting Viva Antiqua does not delete videos already transferred there.

Google account information and YouTube permissions are used to provide the features you request, not for advertising or sale. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections.

Storage, service providers and security

Information is stored on your device and, where required for account, synchronization and online features, on our servers. Hosting, authentication, payment, AI and destination-platform providers process information needed for their functions. Information may be processed outside your country. We use authenticated access and encrypted network connections; no system can guarantee absolute security.

We keep account and feature data while needed to provide the service. Account deletion removes or de-identifies associated records through the service’s deletion process. Certain billing, security and moderation records may remain where needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention or dispute handling. Copies in backups may persist beyond deletion from active systems; deletion from active systems is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup.

Sharing limits and security records

To prevent repeated extraction of recordings, sharing is limited to three newly issued excerpts per account and per device in a rolling 24-hour period, shared across platforms. Stories end at a complete sentence within 60 seconds; other supported formats may finish the sentence that began before 60 seconds, up to 75 seconds. Original recording URLs and decryption keys are not provided through sharing.

We retain purpose-specific, pseudonymous account and device identifiers and hourly timestamp/count summaries for excerpt issuance, share-file download requests, offline playback-license issuance, account deletion and device removal for 90 days from each event. These security records remain for that period after account deletion or removal of a registered device, then expire automatically. They do not contain your email address, sign-in tokens, raw IP address or audio recordings. Pseudonymous identifiers can still link related activity and are not anonymous. This information is used for service security and enforcing sharing limits, not advertising.

The app keeps an installation identifier in the device Keychain to help recognize reinstalls. This is not a permanent hardware identifier and recognition is not guaranteed after device resets or changes to system storage. Share excerpts are available for authenticated re-download for 24 hours and removed from active server storage afterwards, or when the account is deleted. Already exported copies in Photos or destination platforms are outside our deletion controls. A download request or an offline-license issuance does not by itself prove a completed download or a public social-media post.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile, manage device permissions and notifications, revoke connected-platform access, withdraw optional recording consent and request account deletion in the app’s account settings. Deleting the app alone does not delete your server account or cancel an Apple subscription. You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify account ownership without asking for your password.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. The website does not include our own advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice or request consent where appropriate for material changes.
