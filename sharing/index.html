Share a reading video

Viva Antiqua helps you read and listen to Latin and Ancient Greek. Book sharing turns a passage you select into a short reading video with the passage text and audio.

From a book to a video

  1. Open a book and its listening options.
  2. Select a continuous excerpt of complete listening units.
  3. Choose TikTok, YouTube or Instagram before preparing the video.
  4. Tap Export to create the video and continue to your selected destination.

Selection uses downloaded default playback timings, including character names. Stories allow up to 60 seconds; other formats may finish the last sentence up to 75 seconds. Actual video length follows your playback settings. Selecting an excerpt is free; issuing a new export counts toward the shared limit of three per 24 hours.

TikTok: finish your post in TikTok

This integration is being prepared for testing and review. Public TikTok availability has not been approved.

Choose TikTok and tap Export. Connect your TikTok account when needed; Viva Antiqua uploads the video directly to TikTok without saving it to Photos. Once the upload is processed, open your TikTok inbox to edit the video, choose your audience and finish posting. Sending a video does not automatically publish it.

Your content and account

Share only material you have the right to share, including the text, translation and audio. You can cancel before sending. TikTok controls the content after it receives the video; removing Viva Antiqua access does not remove transferred videos.

External sharing uses the destination platform’s authorization. It does not create a post in an in-app community. Play community features are unavailable in the current release configuration.

Other destinations

YouTube and Instagram sharing integrations are also in development. Availability depends on the device, platform configuration and required approvals.
