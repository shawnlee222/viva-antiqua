Help & account choices

Support

This site is being prepared for launch. A public support email will be added before release.

Account deletion

Use the account deletion option in the app’s account settings. Optional pronunciation recording contributions can also be withdrawn separately.

Purchases

For purchases made through Apple, manage your subscription through your Apple account. Include the app version and a description of the issue when contacting support; never send passwords or authorization tokens.

Social sharing

Platform connections are still being tested. An uploaded TikTok draft is completed in TikTok, and YouTube upload visibility is selected before submission. Public availability will follow the required platform approvals.
